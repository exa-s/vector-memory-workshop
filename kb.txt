STORE OVERVIEW
ShopSmart is a fictional online store selling home, kitchen, and personal technology products. Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time. Customers can email help@shopsmart.example. The support team normally replies within one business day.

SHIPPING POLICY
Standard delivery costs ₹99 and usually takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery costs ₹249 and usually takes 1–2 business days after dispatch. Orders over ₹2,500 qualify for free standard delivery. Delivery estimates are business-day estimates and can be affected by destination PIN code or courier delays. Customers receive a tracking link by email after dispatch.

RETURNS AND REFUNDS
Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. To start a return, email help@shopsmart.example with the order ID and reason. After the returned item passes inspection, a refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Shipping charges are not refundable unless ShopSmart sent the wrong or a damaged item. Opened personal-care products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. A return request after the 14-day window is not eligible under the standard return policy.

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS
If an item arrives damaged or the wrong product was delivered, contact support within 48 hours of delivery and include the order ID and clear photos of the item and packaging. ShopSmart will arrange a replacement or refund after reviewing the case. Keep the original packaging until support responds. There is no return shipping charge for an approved damaged-item or incorrect-item claim.
